Homepage Cat · Vanilla JavaScript / SVG

A little life
on your page.

A tiny companion for personal websites. Click to wake it,
pick it up, and let your words become its playground.

01   Wake a sleepy friend.

Click the cat. It stretches, walks along the divider, and settles down.

02   Pick it up. Anywhere.

Drag its head, body, or tail — each has its own dangling pose.

03   Your words are stepping stones.

Drop it onto text. Watch it find its footing and jump home.
